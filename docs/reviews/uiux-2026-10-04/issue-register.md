## Limits that must not become invented defects

No verified contrast failure, missing control label, absent keyboard flashcard support, app-wide console error, actual performance regression, real authorization bypass or excessive gradient/glow pattern was established. No-match search was partially observed; no empty-state defect is asserted. Dark mode uses paired semantic tokens; its restrained contrast is not inherently a defect. No physical mobile keyboard, 200% zoom or screen-reader result is claimed. Timed quiz behavior needs a dedicated assessment before judging its time-limit accessibility.

The 45 issues are intentionally distinct but linked. U01/U02/U03 share foundations; U04/U33 share queue grouping; U09/U43 share resource composition. Estimates overlap and must not be added as independent tasks. Top-ten batches in the main audit consolidate them.
