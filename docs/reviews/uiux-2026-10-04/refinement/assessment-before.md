# Refinement brief and adversarial assessment — 5 October 2026

⚠️ DEGRADED: single-context (the user requested one agent; no independent assessors).

Written before the second concept build. Scope is proposal documentation and a disconnected review prototype. Ahmed has explicitly withheld implementation approval. The original 45 issues, live coverage, scores and limitations remain authoritative evidence of the current product. No new score is promised.

## Where the first proposal failed its own ambition

1. **The shell was interchangeable.** Grey sidebar, white content and blue controls could belong to almost any SaaS tool. Compactness helped geometry but supplied little brand authorship. Retain Open Folio, Manrope, Noto Sans Arabic and the blue accent; give navigation, reading and evidence different structural jobs.
2. **The five layouts still converged.** The working-grid pattern appeared in chat, studio and operations. Distinguish an indexed subject collection, a reading surface with an annotation margin, a configuration/result workspace and an operational master/detail view. Shared controls do not require identical compositions.
3. **Icons were placeholders.** Unicode marks varied in shape, baseline and platform rendering. Replace them with a coordinated 24-unit SVG set with 1.75-unit strokes. Mirror directional arrows in RTL; never mirror the logo.
4. **Comparison content was unfair.** A proposed resumed subject, supported answer, generated flashcard or queued file was compared with an empty current state. That conflated content maturity with design quality. Use the five observed starting states; demonstrate populated states separately.
5. **Motion had no authored product moment.** Generic short fades plus a flashcard turn were insufficient. Make citation-to-source continuity the signature interaction, and design interruption, reversal, focus return and reduced-motion behavior together. Administrative confirmation must stay sober.
6. **Coverage stopped too early.** Five flagship screens do not establish a system. Add working account and shared-state specimens, then specify adoption for every observed screen family, including public errors, recovery, rejected requests and blocked administrative actions.
7. **Dark and Arabic were examples, not a coherent language.** Show real mirrored composition, translated labels, mixed-direction identifiers, Arabic line-height and dark evidence surfaces. Preserve English output when the interface locale is Arabic; these are independent settings.

## Chosen refinement

**Open Folio: reading and annotation.** This extends the existing identity rather than replacing it. A dark navigation spine frames neutral working surfaces; an index carries subjects; a reading margin connects claims to material; a precise state ledger carries administrative decisions. Depth communicates containment and occlusion, not decoration. No gradients, glow, texture, book-flip page entrances or additional libraries.

Physical setting: a student studies for a sustained session on a laptop or phone, sometimes in a dim room; a leader or administrator works through consequential records. Both themes need legible continuous surfaces and unmistakable task boundaries.

## Review contract

- Preserve evidence-backed functional changes: visible chat and studio actions, effective intake deadline, explicit queue choice, reason-led recovery, persistent role/context, truthful receipts and accessible focus behavior.
- Treat visual deltas separately: composition, surface hierarchy, index geometry, SVG icons, typesetting and motion authorship.
- Before/after means the original live capture versus a browser-rendered local proposal, with content/state differences disclosed.
- The prototype simulates fixed synthetic outcomes only. It cannot prove live authorization, provider behavior, persistence, network performance or WCAG conformance.
- Finish with two bounded visual review batches, interaction checks, an acceptance matrix and a founder approval stop. Canonical `DESIGN.md` and application code remain unchanged.
