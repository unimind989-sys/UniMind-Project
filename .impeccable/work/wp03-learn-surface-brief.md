# WP03 student catalog and workspace foundation

## Scope and mode

- Primary target: `src/app/learn/page.tsx`, with the unit workspace and representative Batch Leader/admin structures as related surfaces.
- Visitor mode: **Operate**. Students must locate an authorized curriculum unit and enter its evidence-aware workspace quickly; expression may not obscure hierarchy, state, or familiar controls.

## Audience, job, action, and proof

- Students use UniMind equally on phones and desktop/tablet screens, moving between quick study access and longer focused sessions.
- The primary action is to focus an authorized Module or Subject, understand its readiness/source/quota state, and open its workspace without losing the validated catalog path.
- Product-specific proof is visible authorization-safe scope, derived availability, approved-source readiness, configuration-driven Module/Subject terminology, and locale-switchable English/Arabic behavior with one active interface language at a time.
- Batch Leaders and admins inherit the same visual system for campaign-scoped submissions and auditable governance actions; their permissions and high-impact states remain distinct.

## Constraints and anti-goals

- Support English, Arabic, mixed-direction technical/medical text, RTL/LTR, keyboard, touch, screen readers, text zoom, reduced motion, long labels, minimum/typical/maximum synthetic counts, and no horizontal page overflow.
- Availability and scope are server-derived; browser state and query parameters are never authorization authorities. Hidden/private options must not leak through loading, error, disabled, or analytics states.
- Use semantic local components and project CSS initially. Add an accessible primitive dependency only when native controls cannot support a complex interaction and after provenance, license, RTL, bundle, maintenance, and rollback review.
- No generic LMS dashboard, hospital-software sterility, chatbot-first shell, gamification, streaming-brand imitation, autoplay, entertainment posters, glassmorphism, neon glow, remote runtime assets, invented claims, real private data, or paid services.
