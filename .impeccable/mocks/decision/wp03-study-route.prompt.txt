Subject: a student navigating the authorized catalog path Education stage → Institution → Program → Academic level → Term → Cohort → Curriculum unit, with Human Medicine displaying “Modules”. Composition/framing: The first viewport is one continuous route, not a sidebar-dashboard and not a card grid. A strong horizontal route line crosses the desktop canvas with large labeled bilingual stations. The selected unit station expands downward into a spacious workspace showing safe source readiness, 12 approved sources, a clearly labeled “Open workspace” primary action, and small calibrated quota/source/status readouts. Include enough adjacent structural hints to show the same system can serve Batch Leader campaigns and admin governance without turning them into tables. Use real bilingual interface labels such as “Your study route”, Arabic “مسار دراستك”, “Modules”, Arabic “الوحدات”, “Ready”, Arabic “جاهز”. Keep Arabic correctly rendered and RTL within its labels; isolate English medical terms cleanly.
Style/medium: exceptionally polished production UI mockup; enamel wayfinding plates, satin printed map paper, precise route bands, restrained modern typography, no illustration hero.
Interaction implication: allowed route nodes are solid and connected; unavailable or hidden nodes are not exposed. Keyboard focus and selected states are clearly visible by shape and outline, not color alone.
Constraints: equal credibility on phone and desktop even though this comp is desktop; accessible contrast; familiar controls; serious academic tone; synthetic data only; no claims; no real university logos; no tiny illegible body text; no browser chrome; no device frame; no watermark.
Avoid: generic LMS dashboard, hospital software, chatbot-first layout, gamification, glassmorphism, gradients, oversized marketing headline, floating decorative cards, stock icons, transit-brand parody.
