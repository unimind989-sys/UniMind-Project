What happens if the upper trunk of the brachial plexus is injured?

An upper trunk injury damages the C5 and C6 roots: Erb’s palsy. It usually follows a fall on the shoulder or a difficult delivery that pulls the head away from the shoulder .

The muscles supplied by C5 and C6 weaken: deltoid, supraspinatus, infraspinatus, biceps and brachialis . The arm hangs adducted and medially rotated, with the forearm extended and pronated, the “waiter’s tip” position .

Sensation is lost over the lateral arm and forearm .
