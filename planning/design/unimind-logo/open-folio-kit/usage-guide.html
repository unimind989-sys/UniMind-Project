Open Folio / usage guide

Approved identifier: two open pages, one clear name.

Choose the right file

Use horizontal in headers, stacked on covers, and symbol-only in compact spaces. Light is for white/paper backgrounds; dark for night. Black, white and blue supply one-color options. SVG is the editable master; PNG is the fallback.

Give it room

Keep 32 master units of outside clear space beyond the artboard. At 200 px horizontal width this is 6.8 px per side. Platform tiles already include safe margins.

Use white on active/focused surfaces where cobalt blends into the background. Place the logo on a quiet solid field. RGB masters are for screens; a printer must convert and proof against its target profile. Print minima are starting points, not physical proof. No Pantone match is specified.

English and Arabic

Keep the Latin logo intact, with the book on the left and blue page on the right. Align its container using logical CSS in RTL; never mirror the artwork or reorder letters. Below 144 px available width, use the symbol with an accessible UniMind name.

Preserve the drawing

Scale uniformly. Keep exact colors, spacing and supplied lockups. Use the outlined wordmark instead of retyping it. Avoid shadows, gradients, extra outlines and rotation. The pure-white reverse is optically lighter; the small symbol has a wider gutter.
