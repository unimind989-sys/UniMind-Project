## Brief

UniMind helps students study from authorized, approved material through one knowledge pool per curriculum unit. PRODUCT.md and master-plan sections 6.1–6.2 supply that product meaning; DESIGN.md supplies the calm Study Shelf, matte geometry, Manrope/Noto Sans Arabic interface typography, and restrained cobalt voice.

Audience: university and Thanaweya Amma students, initially Human Medicine and Veterinary Medicine, with English/Arabic and mixed-language study. Adjectives: calm, precise, grounded, connected, academic. Avoid hospital symbolism, chatbot bubbles, brains, mortarboards, sparkles and decorative AI networks.

Assumptions: retain the exact Latin brand spelling UniMind; the symbol must travel between LTR/RTL contexts without mirroring; no Arabic brand spelling is assumed. Show black first. Custom outlined geometric lettering is exploratory and uses no third-party font. UI font choices remain unchanged. Cobalt/night colors are existing UI context, not a newly approved brand palette; mint remains a readiness signal.

Deliverable: three distinct concepts in one sheet, each with a horizontal name treatment and true 64/32/16 px examples. Stop at concept selection before kit work.

## Inspection and results

Round 1 established silhouettes and exploratory lettering. Round 2 introduced the 45-degree U rim, centered its footprint, increased the shelf bars from 24 to 32 units, and enlarged the shared-center square from 32 to 48 units. Round 3 refined the wordmark M to exact 60-degree diagonals, resolving the audit's near-angle warnings. Earlier drawings and overview sheets remain available for comparison. `build-concepts.py` reconstructs the versioned SVGs without fonts or network access.

Final files: `a-sourcefold-v3.svg`, `b-focus-shelf-v3.svg`, `c-common-ground-v3.svg`, their exploratory `-lockup-v3.svg` files, and `concepts.png`/`concepts.svg`. All six final artwork files passed the SVG structural audit without warnings: one flat color, no live text, embedded raster, filters or strokes. The heuristic 100/100 audit is a structural result, not a brand or legal judgment.

Rendered and inspected rounds 1, 2 and 3. Inspected the internal preview for 16/24/32 px, white-on-dark reversal, blur, mirror, 180-degree rotation, circular crop, header use and equal-height comparison against four library examples. All three basic silhouettes survive the size ladder; A's diagonal is subtle at 16 px, B can read as a layout icon, and C is the most abstract and can suggest a target. White reversal is required on the existing night canvas; black artwork on that canvas is intentionally a failed contrast sample. No production minimum size, final reversed optical cut or legal originality clearance is claimed.

Recommendation: A provides the clearest link to the UniMind name with a compact signature. B is closest to the Study Shelf's spatial grammar. C expresses the single shared knowledge pool across source formats. Library comparison is limited to the inspected examples; it is not a comprehensive competitor or trademark search.

Repository checks: secret scan passed for 2598 files. Readiness initially rejected the generator's underscore filename; it was renamed to lowercase kebab-case before repeating the check. Application/build/CI checks are outside this local concept-only checkpoint; no application behavior changed.
